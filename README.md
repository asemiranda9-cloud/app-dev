# 💊 Breaking Bad

## **Overview**
**Breaking Bad** is a critically acclaimed crime drama series created by Vince Gilligan. It follows a high school chemistry teacher turned methamphetamine producer.

> *"I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger."* — Walter White

---

## **Main Characters**
- **Walter White**: A chemistry teacher who becomes a drug kingpin  
- **Jesse Pinkman**: Walter’s former student and business partner  
- **Skyler White**: Walter’s wife, caught between loyalty and fear  
- **Hank Schrader**: DEA agent and Walter’s brother-in-law

---

## **Seasons Table**

| Season | Year | Key Plot Point |
|--------|------|----------------|
| 1      | 2008 | Walter begins cooking meth after cancer diagnosis |
| 2      | 2009 | The empire grows, but consequences mount |
| 3      | 2010 | Introduction of Gus Fring and deeper criminal ties |
| 4      | 2011 | Power struggle with Gus reaches deadly climax |
| 5      | 2012–2013 | Walter’s downfall and final reckoning |

---

## **Why I Love It 🔥**
1. Brilliant character transformation  
2. Intense moral dilemmas  
3. Sharp writing and cinematography  
4. Iconic performances by Bryan Cranston and Aaron Paul
